VIVAIATHAN
OPERATOR SYSTEM DESIGN
LOG No. 001
STATUS: INITIAL DESIGN SPECIFICATION

============================================================
1. CORE STRUCTURE
============================================================

Playable Operators are defined by two independent classifications:

- ROLE: Defines the Operator's primary battlefield responsibility,
  stat tendencies, and tactical contribution.
- ATTRIBUTE: Defines the behavior of selected special abilities,
  special equipment, and attribute-based effects.

Each Operator has exactly one Role and one Attribute.
Role and Attribute are independent; the same Role may exist with
different Attributes, and the same Attribute may appear across
different Roles.

Neither Role nor Attribute should be treated as a rarity level.

============================================================
2. ROLES
============================================================

[ASSAULT]
Purpose:
  Frontline offense and reliable general combat.

Strengths:
  - Strong weapon handling and sustained combat performance.
  - Good balance of mobility and offensive capability.
  - Suitable for players who engage threats directly.

Limitations:
  - Limited healing, reconnaissance, and deployment utility.
  - Should not outperform specialist Roles in their own fields.

Design direction:
  An accessible, flexible combat Operator. Strong in direct
  engagements without becoming the best choice for every situation.


[RECON]
Purpose:
  Reconnaissance, threat identification, and precision combat.

Strengths:
  - Detects or highlights threats and important targets.
  - Can mark targets to support the whole squad.
  - Excels at precision attacks and identifying weak points.

Limitations:
  - Lower durability or weaker sustained close-range performance.
  - Information skills should require positioning, timing, or cooldowns.

Design direction:
  Creates advantages before and during engagements through
  information and accuracy rather than raw durability.


[MEDIC]
Purpose:
  Recovery, emergency response, and squad sustainment.

Strengths:
  - Restores health or provides temporary protection.
  - Improves recovery and may assist downed teammates.
  - Helps the squad remain operational during long engagements.

Limitations:
  - Lower direct offensive specialization.
  - Healing and revival effects must have clear cooldowns or resource costs.
  - Must not make the squad effectively immortal.

Design direction:
  Provides meaningful recovery without replacing careful play
  or making damage irrelevant.


[ENGINEER]
Purpose:
  Field preparation, deployable equipment, and area control.

Strengths:
  - Deploys traps, defensive devices, or automated equipment.
  - Improves barricades and other defensive structures.
  - Can provide ammunition or repair support if those systems are added.

Limitations:
  - Strongest when given time and a suitable position.
  - Deployables should have limits such as quantity, durability,
    ammunition, or cooldown.
  - Less effective when forced to move constantly.

Design direction:
  Controls the battlefield through preparation and equipment,
  not through unrestricted damage output.


[DEFENDER]
Purpose:
  Frontline protection, damage mitigation, and crowd control.

Strengths:
  - Can reduce incoming damage or protect nearby teammates.
  - Excels at holding narrow routes and protecting objectives.
  - Can interrupt, stagger, or redirect groups of enemies.

Limitations:
  - Lower mobility or slower weapon handling.
  - Protection effects must not provide permanent invulnerability.
  - Should control threats rather than eliminate every threat alone.

Design direction:
  Keeps the squad and its position intact, creating room for
  other Roles to perform their jobs.


============================================================
3. ATTRIBUTES
============================================================

Attribute effects primarily apply to Operator abilities and
equipment explicitly designed to use that Attribute. Standard
firearms do not automatically inherit an Operator's Attribute.

[KINETIC]
Theme:
  Conventional physical force and dependable performance.

Effects:
  - No special status effect by default.
  - Stable and broadly useful against ordinary targets.
  - Serves as the baseline for comparing other Attributes.

Design role:
  Consistency and reliability rather than a specialized counter.


[THERMAL]
Theme:
  Heat, ignition, and high-temperature damage.

Effects:
  - Can inflict a burn effect that deals damage over time.
  - Can be particularly effective against targets that regenerate
    or rely on biological growth, if such enemy types are introduced.

Limitations:
  - Damage over time should not stack without a defined limit.
  - Some targets may resist heat-based effects.

Design role:
  Sustained pressure and anti-regeneration specialization.


[CRYO]
Theme:
  Cooling, freezing, and movement suppression.

Effects:
  - Reduces enemy movement speed.
  - Repeated application may build toward a brief freeze or disable.
  - Useful for controlling fast enemies and groups.

Limitations:
  - Control duration must be limited, especially against bosses.
  - Repeated freezes should have diminishing returns or immunity windows.

Design role:
  Crowd control and safe repositioning, not unrestricted damage.


[ELECTRIC]
Theme:
  Electrical discharge and neural disruption.

Effects:
  - Briefly interrupts or stuns affected enemies.
  - Certain abilities may chain between nearby targets.
  - Useful for disrupting groups or interrupting dangerous actions.

Limitations:
  - Stun duration must remain short and controlled.
  - Bosses and elite enemies should have strong resistance to repeated
    interruption.

Design role:
  Short interruptions and group disruption.


[CORROSIVE]
Theme:
  Chemical degradation and weakening of protective layers.

Effects:
  - Applies corrosion that reduces target protection or defense.
  - Can weaken hardened, plated, or heavily protected enemies.
  - May deal limited damage over time if required by balance.

Limitations:
  - Defense reduction must have a cap and a clear duration.
  - The effect should not make armor or defensive enemies irrelevant.

Design role:
  Weakening durable targets and creating openings for the squad.


============================================================
4. ROLE / ATTRIBUTE SEPARATION
============================================================

The two classifications must remain mechanically distinct.

Examples:
  - ASSAULT + THERMAL:
    An offensive Operator whose special ability applies burn.
  - RECON + ELECTRIC:
    A scouting Operator whose marked-target ability can briefly
    disrupt or expose threats.
  - MEDIC + CRYO:
    A support Operator whose defensive equipment slows approaching
    enemies while assisting squad recovery.
  - ENGINEER + CORROSIVE:
    A deployable-equipment specialist who weakens hardened targets.
  - DEFENDER + KINETIC:
    A protection-focused Operator using reliable, non-elemental effects.

These are combination examples, not finalized characters.

============================================================
5. BALANCE PRINCIPLES
============================================================

1. Every Operator must have one clear primary function.
2. A Role should provide strengths and trade-offs, not universal
   superiority over other Roles.
3. An Attribute should add a tactical effect, not automatically
   increase every weapon's damage.
4. No single Role / Attribute combination should solve every situation.
5. Status effects require limits, durations, cooldowns, or resistance
   rules appropriate to their impact.
6. Rarity should not directly determine whether an Operator is usable.
   Higher rarity should favor distinctive or flexible kits rather than
   simply making lower-rarity Operators obsolete.
7. Squad composition should reward cooperation without forcing one
   mandatory Role or Attribute.
8. Character balance must be evaluated in actual gameplay, including
   solo play and multiplayer environments.
